## Completed assembly

The retained lab summary records completion of:

- Motherboard and AMD Ryzen 5 3600 installation.
- DDR4 memory installation, with 16 GB recorded in the hardware inventory.
- NVMe SSD installation.
- Discrete graphics card installation.
- System power connections.
- Front-fan power and RGB wiring.

The [photo gallery](photo-gallery.md) now shows the motherboard and an AMD-branded CPU cooler mounted in the case, exterior and rear chassis views, PSU cable identification, a mounted PSU, and wiring in progress. The early interior photos show empty DIMM and expansion slots, while the rear chassis photos show an open PSU bay. These earlier states are separate from the later completed assembly reported above.

The exact assembly order, cooler model, PSU model, and final cable routes remain unconfirmed. The mounted PSU photo shows an 80 PLUS Bronze marking; its full model designation is not established from the supplied view.

## Result and verification boundary

After the memory troubleshooting described in the [troubleshooting log](troubleshooting.md), display output was restored and the system reached BIOS. This is the recorded endpoint of the hardware assembly phase.

Measured fan RPM, fan curves, lighting-control behavior, temperatures, and sustained-load results were not retained in the accessible record. Wiring completion does not substitute for those operational checks.
